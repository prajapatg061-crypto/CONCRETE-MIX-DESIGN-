About IS 10262:2019 Concrete Mix Design & Rate Analysis

This civil engineering web application automates concrete mix proportioning in full accordance with IS 10262:2019 and IS 456:2000 standards. Designed for quality control engineers, ready-mix concrete (RMC) plants, and site managers, the calculator converts structural specifications and raw material physical properties into precise saturated surface-dry (SSD) and batch-ready field weights per cubic meter (m³).

Beyond standard mix proportions, the tool incorporates dual coarse aggregate blending (20mm and 10mm), supplementary cementitious materials (Fly Ash or GGBS replacement), chemical admixture dosage (PCE superplasticizers), and automatic field moisture adjustments. Integrated rate analysis provides immediate cost transparency and breakdowns per unit volume.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the calculator perform aggregate moisture corrections?

Field batching requires adjusting SSD weights for free surface moisture and absorption. Water absorbed by aggregates is subtracted, while surface moisture is deducted from mixing water and added to aggregate batching weights.

What are the standard target strength margins in IS 10262:2019?

Target mean strength ($f'_{ck}$) is computed as $f'_{ck} = f_{ck} + 1.65 \times S$, where $S$ is the standard deviation based on the grade of concrete (e.g., 5.0 N/mm² for M30–M50 grades).
